P Foundation Subprocessors

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 · Changelog

Introduction​

P Foundation (“we,” “us,” or “our”) engages a limited number of third-party service providers to help operate our website, programs, and organization. Where one of these providers processes personal information on our behalf, we refer to it as a “subprocessor.” This page lists our current subprocessors and should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which describes the personal information we collect and how we use it.

How We Select Subprocessors​

Before engaging a subprocessor, we review its security and privacy practices. Each subprocessor is bound by a written agreement, typically a data processing agreement, that:

  • Limits Use: Permits processing only on our instructions and only for the purpose listed below.
  • Requires Security: Obliges the provider to maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures.
  • Requires Confidentiality: Binds the provider and its personnel to keep the information confidential.
  • Covers International Transfers: Includes Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent lawful mechanism where personal data leaves the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland.

Network Providers​

P Foundation operates its own network, AS399728, and exchanges traffic with Internet transit and peering providers. These providers carry network traffic between endpoints without access to, or control over, its content, and they do not process personal information on our behalf. For this reason they are not subprocessors and are not listed above.

Changes to This List​

We update this page when we add or replace a subprocessor and revise the “Effective Date” at the top accordingly. For customers under our Master Services Agreement, this page is the register described in Section 3.4 of that Agreement: we give at least 30 days' notice before adding or replacing a provider that delivers Customer Content or processes Customer Data, except for an emergency replacement under Section 3.5, which we notify as soon as practicable.
