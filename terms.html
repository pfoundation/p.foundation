Service Agreements​

Our services are provided under our standard Master Services Agreement. Each service adds its own Service Appendix and any service level agreement. These terms apply to a customer only once that customer and P Foundation sign a Service Order, which records the customer's details, the services ordered, and any agreed variations. A signed Service Order is governed by the version of these terms in force on the date it is signed. To discuss a service, please contact us.

Changes and Verification​

Each document shows the date it was last updated, with a Changelog link to its full edit history on GitHub.

Every published version of these documents is signed with Sigstore and released on GitHub as a signed terms release. Each release contains the documents, their SHA-256 checksums, and a signature bundle recorded in Sigstore's public transparency log. The signature is made by our publishing workflow on the master branch, without a private key that could be lost or copied. To confirm that a copy you hold matches what we published, download the release files and run:

cosign verify-blob SHA256SUMS \
  --bundle SHA256SUMS.sigstore.json \
  --certificate-identity https://github.com/pfoundation/p.foundation/.github/workflows/sign-terms.yml@refs/heads/master \
  --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com
sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
